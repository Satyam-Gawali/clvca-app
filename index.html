Core Features

Engineered for Freedom & Privacy

📶

Offline-First Translation

Download local translation models once and communicate forever. No active internet, cellular connection, or Wi-Fi hotspot required to translate text and voice.

🔗

Peer-to-Peer Voice Chat

Link devices directly using local Bluetooth and Wi-Fi antennas. Speak to nearby friends or partners instantly with minimum latency and zero server reliance.

🔒

Privacy-First Architecture

Your voice and data are entirely yours. Since processing happens entirely on-device, no audio files, transcripts, or conversations are uploaded to any server.

🎙️

High Quality TTS & STT

Integration with high-accuracy speech processing systems. Select different accents, control speech rate, and enjoy realistic synthesized audio readouts.

Interactive Guide

Two Conversation Modes

CLVCA offers two powerful, 100% offline modes tailored to any conversation setup. Share a single device or connect two nearby devices wirelessly.

01

Set Your Languages

Tap the language name at the top of each card to select who speaks what language.

02

Adjust Voice Settings

Tap the 'Tune' icon on any card to change speaking speed, pitch, or regional accents.

03

Tap a Mic to Start

Tap the microphone icon on a speaker's card to start listening. Speak naturally for best results.

04

Manual Text Entry

Use the keyboard icon if you prefer typing. Your message will be translated instantly.

05

Hear the Translation

The translation appears on the opposite card and plays automatically. Tap the speaker icon to replay it.

06

Fullscreen View

Tap the fullscreen icon for a large, rotated display—ideal for showing results to someone sitting across from you.

07

Use Auto Mode

Enable 'Auto' from the top bar for a hands-free conversation. It automatically switches microphone detection between speakers.

01

Wireless Setup

Ensure Bluetooth and Wi-Fi are ON on both devices. No internet is required, just active wireless antennas.

02

Start Hosting

One person taps 'Start Hosting' to become visible. This generates a unique device name for others to search.

03

Scan & Connect

The other person taps 'Join Friend' and scans the Host's QR code or selects their name from the list to pair instantly.

04

Secure Connection

Once paired, a secure offline link is established, and you are both redirected to the private chat room.

05

Personalize Settings

Tap the 'Tune' icon in the chat to select your language, choose a regional accent, or adjust speed and pitch.

06

Voice & Text Input

Use the microphone for real-time speech translation or the keyboard to type. Messages appear instantly on both screens.

07

Translation Quality

A 'Bolt' icon indicates high-quality offline translation. If a 'Download' icon appears, tap to install the local language model.

08

Session History

All your P2P conversations are saved locally. Revisit them anytime from the 'Recent P2P Chats' on the home screen.

09

End Session

Tap the disconnect button to safely end the chat. This closes the wireless P2P link and preserves your privacy.
